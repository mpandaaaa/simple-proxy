  Check the build pill on perfectxi.app reads s67-1 afterwards.


WHAT CHANGED SINCE S66-1
  This one is almost all data accuracy. A football fan would have spotted
  every item below, and several were embarrassing.

  SEVEN CLUB-SEASONS THAT NEVER HAPPENED
    The wheel could land you on Wolves 2002/03 or Norwich 2014/15 — seasons
    those clubs spent in the division below. Removed:
      Crystal Palace 1993/94 and 1998/99, Middlesbrough 1997/98,
      Charlton 1999/00, Wolves 2002/03, Norwich 2014/15, Hull 2015/16
    And Nottingham Forest's 1994/95 side was missing entirely — they
    finished 3rd that year with Collymore scoring 22. Added, with Alfie
    Haaland (Erling's dad) in it.
    The database now holds exactly 706 club-seasons, which is the real
    number: 22 clubs for the first three seasons, 20 ever since.

  TEN PEOPLE WHO WERE NEVER PLAYERS
    Mostly the club's MANAGER, sitting in the squad as a low-rated player:
      Kevin Keegan (Newcastle 93/94), Martin O'Neill (Leicester 98/99),
      Peter Reid (Sunderland 96/97), Martin Jol (Spurs 04/05),
      Glenn Roeder (West Ham 02/03), Steve Kean (Blackburn 11/12),
      Billy Davies (Derby 07/08), Owen Coyle (Burnley 09/10)
    Plus Andrea Radrizzani, who owned Leeds and never played football,
    and "Gael Bale", who is not a person.

  TWELVE PLAYERS STORED TWICE UNDER A REVERSED NAME
    Joe Cole and "Cole Joe". Ian Rush and "Rush Ian". Son Heung-min and
    "Heung-min Son". Also Shaka Hislop, Shola Ameobi, Andy Carroll,
    Solly March, Paulo Wanchope, Dean Marney, Pajtim Kasami.
    Two of them — Lee Chung-yong at Bolton and Sun Jihai at Man City —
    were in the SAME squad under both spellings, so you could draft the
    same player twice into one XI.

  PINCH-ZOOM RESTORED ON 20 PAGES
    Every page blocked zooming. That fails an accessibility standard and
    made the guides hard to read on a phone.

  COUNTS CORRECTED EVERYWHERE
    The site said 3,003 players and 712 club-seasons. It is now 2,978 and
    706, which is what the database actually contains.


A WEEKLY CHECK NOW RUNS ON ITS OWN
  Every Monday morning it looks at the live database, clears any stuck
  multiplayer rooms, checks the site is up, researches one thing, and
  sends you a short note. You do not need to start it.
