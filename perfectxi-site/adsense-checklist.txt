NOW DO THIS (the part that actually decides approval)

1. DEPLOY everything, then hard-refresh the site twice.

2. GOOGLE SEARCH CONSOLE - diagnose indexing. This is the likely blocker.
   - Add/verify perfectxi.app if not already.
   - Sitemaps -> submit  sitemap.xml
   - URL Inspection on EACH of these, note the status, then "Request Indexing":
       /                        /guides.html            /how-to-play.html
       /faq.html                /about.html             /contact.html
       /changelog.html          /guides/match-engine.html
       /guides/dynasty-guide.html   /guides/chemistry.html
     (roughly 10-15 requests per day is the informal limit)
   - Read the Pages report. "Discovered - currently not indexed" = crawl
     priority problem (needs links). "Crawled - currently not indexed" =
     quality signal problem.

3. LIVE TEST what Google sees: in URL Inspection use "Test Live URL" ->
   "View Tested Page" -> HTML. Confirm you can see the words
   "Perfect XI is a free English top-flight draft game". If you can, the
   reviewer can too.

4. INDEXNOW (optional, helps Bing fast):
   POST the contents of indexnow-submit.json to https://api.indexnow.org/indexnow
   with Content-Type: application/json

5. GET 2-3 REAL BACKLINKS. New sites with none get crawled slowly.
   r/FantasyPL, r/soccer weekly threads, r/incremental_games, a football
   forum, itch.io, or a Product Hunt / Hacker News post.

6. WAIT 3-4 WEEKS. Do not reapply before your key pages show as INDEXED.
   Reapplying early just re-shows Google the old site and burns a review.

7. THEN in AdSense: Sites -> perfectxi.app -> Request Review.

IF IT FAILS A THIRD TIME
  The residual blocker is the game-on-the-homepage. Options, in order:
   a) Make a text page the root and move the game to /play (biggest change,
      highest success rate for game sites).
   b) Apply to AdinPlay/Venatus instead - built for browser games, and they
      pay better for gameplay traffic than AdSense does anyway.
   c) Publish on CrazyGames for rewarded video revenue.
