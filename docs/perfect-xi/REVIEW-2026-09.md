## Issue 1. Guideline 2.5.4, Performance, Software Requirements

> The app declares support for audio in the UIBackgroundModes key in the
> Info.plist, but we are unable to play any audible content when the app is
> running in the background.

Perfect XI has no feature that needs audio in the background. Sound effects
play only while the app is open, which needs no background mode.

**Fix:** remove `audio` from `UIBackgroundModes`. In an Expo project it comes
from one of:

- `ios.infoPlist.UIBackgroundModes` in app.json or app.config
- an audio config plugin option such as expo-audio's background playback
  setting, or expo-av configured for background playback

Remove the key entirely if `audio` was its only entry. Check the built
Info.plist, not just app.json, because config plugins add keys at build time.

## Issue 2. Guideline 2.1, Information Needed (App Tracking Transparency)

> The app uses the AppTrackingTransparency framework, but we are unable to
> locate the App Tracking Transparency permission request.

Perfect XI does not track users: no ads, no accounts, no data shared with
brokers or linked with third party data for advertising.

**Fix: remove tracking, do not add the prompt.**

- Remove `expo-tracking-transparency` and any `requestTrackingPermissions`
  call.
- Remove `NSUserTrackingUsageDescription` from the Info.plist config.
- Remove any SDK that links AppTrackingTransparency.framework: ad SDKs
  (react-native-google-mobile-ads, AdMob), attribution SDKs (AppsFlyer,
  Adjust, Branch), Facebook SDK, and analytics configured to collect the
  advertising identifier.
- **App Store Connect, App Privacy:** change "Do you or your third-party
  partners use data for tracking purposes?" to **No**. Needs the Account
  Holder or Admin role, so Mo does this.
